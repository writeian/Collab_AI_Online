About AI Collab Teams

Empowering collaborative learning through AI-assisted writing

🎯 Our Mission

AI Collab Teams transforms how students and instructors work together on writing projects. By combining the power of AI assistance with structured collaboration, we help teams develop better writing skills through guided, interactive learning experiences.

✨ Key Features

🏠 Room-Based Collaboration

Create dedicated spaces for your teams, classes, or projects. Each room provides a focused environment for collaborative writing with member management and permissions.

🤖 AI Writing Assistant

Powered by advanced AI models (Anthropic Claude and OpenAI GPT-4o), providing intelligent feedback and guidance throughout the writing process.

📝 Dynamic Learning Steps

Learning steps are automatically generated based on room learning goals, providing contextual AI assistance. Instructors can customize these steps to match their specific teaching objectives.

📊 Instructor Dashboard

Comprehensive analytics and management tools for instructors to track student progress, monitor AI usage patterns, and manage custom prompts.

💬 Interactive Comments

Students can comment on specific dialogue points, creating threaded discussions around AI feedback and collaborative learning.

🔗 Google Docs Integration

Import and analyze Google Docs content directly into your collaborative sessions for AI-powered feedback and improvement.

🎯 Custom Prompts

Instructors can create and customize AI prompts for specific learning steps and rooms, tailoring the AI assistance to their teaching goals.

📈 Learning Analytics

Track student engagement, writing mode preferences, and AI interaction patterns to optimize the learning experience.

🎓 Educational Focus

AI Collab Teams is specifically designed for educational environments, helping students develop critical writing skills through:

  • Structured Learning: Guided writing processes with clear stages and objectives
  • Collaborative Feedback: Team-based review and discussion of writing
  • AI-Assisted Learning: Intelligent guidance that adapts to each writing stage
  • Progress Tracking: Detailed analytics for instructors and students

👨‍🏫 For Instructors: Google Docs Integration

Help your students get AI feedback on their actual documents with our Google Docs integration:

📋 Setup Instructions

  1. Get Service Account Email: Contact your system administrator for the Google service account email (format: ai-collab-docs@project-id.iam.gserviceaccount.com)
  2. Share Documents: Students share their Google Docs with the service account email, setting permissions to "Viewer" or "Commenter"
  3. Copy Sharing Link: Students copy the sharing link from their Google Doc
  4. Create Chat: When starting a new chat, paste the Google Doc URL in the designated field
  5. Get AI Analysis: The AI will analyze the document and provide writing feedback

🎯 Best Practices in Collab AI

  • Use different learning steps (Explore, Draft, Revise, etc.) for different stages of document development
  • Encourage students to ask specific questions about their writing
  • Monitor student progress through the instructor dashboard
  • Use the commenting system to provide additional guidance on AI feedback

📋 Best Practices in Google Docs

  • Document Length: Collab AI works best with documents that are 500-1,500 words. Anything over 3,000 words may impact performance
  • Tab Limitations: Only the first tab of a document will be read
  • Long Documents: Students should break longer essays into sections organized by tabs. Collab AI will only read the first tab
  • Troubleshooting: If documents fail to load, check that they're shared with the correct service account email

📊 Track Progress

  • View student analytics in your instructor dashboard
  • Monitor which learning steps students use most
  • Track AI usage patterns and student engagement
  • Customize AI prompts for different learning steps and rooms
