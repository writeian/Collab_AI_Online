AI Collaboration, Reimagined

Non-profit & Open Source

AI tools remain black boxes, positioned just outside the circle of learning and collaboration. Students and teammates step into isolated chatrooms, but their process remains invisible to instructors, leaders, and peers.

What if AI were no longer used as a black box but a shared tool inside the circle of learning and collaboration?

To do so, let's first take off the black box's lid to see what everyone is doing.

Next, let's remove some of the walls to allow communication and collaboration.

Let's shape the collaborative space to improve quality, safety, and privacy.

Finally, let's bring teams together.
