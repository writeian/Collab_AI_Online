AI Collaboration, Reimagined

Non-profit & Open Source

AI tools remain opaque, closed rooms, positioned just outside the circle of learning and collaboration. Students and teammates step into isolated chatrooms and their process remains invisible to instructors, leaders, and peers.

What if AI were no longer used as an isolated workroom but a shared tool inside the circle of learning and collaboration?

To do so, let's first open the door to the room to share what we are doing.

Next, let's remove some of the walls to allow communication and collaboration.

Let's shape the collaborative space to improve quality, safety, and privacy.

Finally, let's bring teams together.
